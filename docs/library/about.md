# chainladder-python Team Members

The chainladder-python team is a diverse group of actuaries, developers, students, and researchers from across the profession and around the world. If you are interested in contributing to the project, please reach out to the team.

The development team currently functions as part of the CAS Open-Source Projects Working Group.

## Contact

To reach us, please email us at [chainladder@casact.org](mailto:chainladder@casact.org).
